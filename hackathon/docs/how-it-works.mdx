# How the hackathon works

## 1. Understand the challenge

Review the problem brief, learn about spaza shop payment challenges and
identify a focused problem your team wants to address.

## 2. Define your solution

Develop your concept, users, use cases and proposed payment journey.

## 3. Build a proof of concept

Use the provided technology, documentation and support sessions to create a
functional or partially functional prototype.

## 4. Present your solution

Submit your project and pitch it to the judging panel, explaining the problem,
solution, technical implementation and potential impact.
