## Registration details

- **Registration deadline:** 26 August
- **Places:** limited to 15 teams - accepted on a first come, first served
  basis once registration has been reviewed
- **Team size:** 3–5 participants (at least one technical member); individuals
  can register and be placed into teams
- **Participation cost:** to be confirmed
- **Format:** online hackathon - teams collaborate remotely or in person as
  they prefer, with the top teams pitching in person at the finale in Cape
  Town (venue to be confirmed)
