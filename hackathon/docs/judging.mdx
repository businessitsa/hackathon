# Judging criteria

| Criterion | Weighting | What we're looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Understanding the Problem** | 20% | Demonstrates a clear understanding of the challenges faced by spaza shops and defines a meaningful problem to solve. |
| **Solution Design & Innovation** | 20% | A creative, well-reasoned solution that addresses the problem effectively. Judges will consider the team's design process, decision making, and how the solution evolved. |
| **Use of Open Payments & Interoperability** | 20% | Effective use of open payment technologies, APIs, standards, or Interledger concepts where appropriate. |
| **Technical Implementation** | 15% | Quality of the implementation, architecture, code, prototype, or proof of concept. The solution should demonstrate technical feasibility. |
| **User Experience & Accessibility** | 10% | The solution is intuitive, accessible, and designed with the end user in mind. |
| **Impact & Feasibility** | 10% | The solution has the potential to make a meaningful difference for spaza shops and could realistically be adopted or expanded. |
| **Presentation & Demo** | 5% | Clear communication of the problem, solution, implementation, and overall project during the recorded presentation and demo. |

## Judging philosophy

:::tip
Great hackathon projects aren't just about the final product - they're about
the journey. Judges will evaluate how teams approached the problem, explored
ideas, justified their decisions, and demonstrated their solution. Whether
your project is a polished prototype or a technical proof of concept, we're
looking for thoughtful problem solving, innovation, and real-world impact.
:::
