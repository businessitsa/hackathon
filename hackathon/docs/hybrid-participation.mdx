# Join from Cape Town or online

This is a hybrid event — participants may join in person or online.

## In person

Attend the opening, working sessions, mentor support and final presentations
at the Cape Town venue.

## Online

Join the opening session, workshops, mentor check-ins and presentations
through the event's online platforms.

## The same experience for every team

All teams receive access to the same:

- Challenge brief
- Technical documentation
- Workshops
- Mentor support
- Communication channels
- Submission process
- Judging criteria

:::note Remote final presentations
Whether final presentations can also be delivered remotely will be confirmed
before the event — check back here or ask in the event communication
channels.
:::
