# Schedule

Teams will have **30 days** to design, build, and refine their solutions. The
hackathon is designed to give participants the flexibility to work remotely,
collaborate on their own schedule, and engage with mentors throughout the
build period.

| Phase | Timeline | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 1 - Registration** | 14–26 August | Register as an individual or team. Once registration closes, participants will receive access to the Discord community, team channels, resources, and mentor information. |
| **Phase 2 - Build Period** | 1–30 September | Over the next 30 days, teams will work on their solutions remotely at their own pace. Mentors will be available throughout the month via Discord to answer questions and provide guidance. Teams may also schedule an optional mid-project check-in to discuss progress, receive feedback, and validate their ideas before the final submission. |
| **Phase 3 - Project Submission & Judging** | 1 October | Submit your completed project through your team's Discord channel. The judging panel will review all submissions and select the top five teams to advance to the Grand Finale. |
| **Phase 4 - Grand Finale** | 24 October | The five finalist teams will present their solutions live to the judging panel at the in-person Grand Finale in Cape Town. Following the presentations, the winning teams will be announced and prizes awarded. |

:::note
Venue details for the Grand Finale will be announced closer to the event.
:::
