# Schedule

The hackathon runs as a **4-week online event**, ending with a final
in-person judging session in Cape Town.

| Phase | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Register your team by **26 August** |
| Week 1 - Understand | Opening session, challenge brief walkthrough and an introduction to Interledger and Open Payments |
| Week 2 - Define | Develop your concept, users, use cases and proposed payment journey, with workshops and mentor check-ins |
| Weeks 3–4 - Build | Build your proof of concept with technical support sessions |
| Finale | Submit your project and pitch it at the final in-person judging event, with prizes and swag |

:::note
Detailed dates and times for each session will be announced here closer to
the event.
:::
