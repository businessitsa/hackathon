# Who can participate?

Teams can be multidisciplinary - you don't need to be a developer to take
part. The hackathon is open to:

- Software developers
- UX and UI designers
- Product designers
- Fintech professionals
- Students and early-career technologists
- Entrepreneurs and community innovators
- Payment and financial technology enthusiasts

## Team size

Teams should ideally include **three to five participants**, with at least one
person able to work on the technical implementation.

:::note[Registering as an individual?]
If you don't have a team yet, you can still register - we will do our best to
place individuals into teams. Indicate on the registration form that you are
registering as an individual.
:::
