# Mentors and judges

## Mentors

Mentors support teams throughout the hackathon with technical guidance,
product feedback and payment domain expertise. Teams are encouraged to
schedule an optional midway check-in with a mentor - see
[Mentor support](./technology.mdx#mentor-support) for details.

:::note
Mentor profiles will be announced soon.
:::

## Judges

Judges assess the final submissions against the published
[judging criteria](./judging.mdx).

:::note
Judge profiles will be announced soon.
:::
