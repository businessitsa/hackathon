# Submission requirements

Please submit the following as your final hackathon entry:

### 1. Presentation

A presentation (PDF or PowerPoint) covering:

- The problem statement and opportunity
- Research and key insights (if applicable)
- User stories, use cases, or user journeys
- Information architecture, user flows, system architecture, or technical
  diagrams (where relevant)
- Design decisions and solution process
- Technical implementation and technology stack
- Key features and functionality
- Challenges encountered and how they were overcome
- Expected impact and future improvements

### 2. Recorded presentation

A **10–15 minute recorded presentation** walking the judges through your
project, demonstrating your solution, and explaining your design and
technical decisions.

### 3. Working demo, prototype, or source repository

A working demo, prototype, or source repository, submitted as a **live link**
for the judges to review.

:::warning
Remember that each project should demonstrate a meaningful connection to
Interledger or Open Payments technology - see the
[technical requirement](./technology.mdx#important-technical-requirement).
:::

A submission template will be linked from the
[Technology & Resources](./technology.mdx) page once it is ready.

## Finalists and the Grand Finale

The judging panel will review all submissions and select the **top five
teams** based on the [judging criteria](./judging.mdx). These finalists will
be invited to present their solutions live at the in-person Grand Finale,
where the winners will be announced.
