# Technology and resources

## Core technology

Teams will be introduced to:

- [Interledger Protocol](https://interledger.org/developers/get-started/)
- [Open Payments](https://openpayments.dev/)
- [Rafiki](https://rafiki.dev/)
- Payment pointers and wallet addresses
- Relevant APIs and sandbox environments

## Supporting tools

Teams may also use:

- JavaScript or TypeScript
- Node.js
- React, Next.js or another front-end framework
- GitHub
- Figma
- Postman
- Database and hosting tools of their choice

## Important technical requirement

:::warning
Each project should demonstrate a **meaningful connection to Interledger or
Open Payments technology**. A standard payment application with no clear use
of the provided technology would not meet the core challenge requirements.
:::

## Discord community

Throughout the hackathon, teams are encouraged to stay connected through our
dedicated Discord community. This is the main communication hub for the
event, where participants can ask questions, share ideas, receive
announcements, and connect with other teams.

**Join the Hackathon Discord Server:**
[Join the Cape Town Software Development Meetup Discord Server!](https://discord.gg/xtRb2UK9k)

## Mentor support

Our hackathon mentors will be available throughout the challenge to provide
guidance, answer questions, and help teams overcome technical or design
challenges. While mentors are there to support your progress, they won't
build your solution or make decisions on your behalf.

Teams are encouraged to schedule midway check-in sessions with a mentor to
discuss their progress, validate ideas, and receive feedback. These check-ins
are recommended but entirely optional, and are intended to help teams stay on
track before the final submission.

Whether you need help understanding the challenge, exploring open payment
technologies, or refining your solution, our mentors and community are here
to support you throughout the hackathon.
