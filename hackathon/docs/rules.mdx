# Rules

The most important points are summarised below. The full competition rules
and code of conduct will be linked here once published.

1. Teams must register before the deadline.
2. Work should primarily be completed during the official hackathon period.
3. Existing open-source tools may be used but must be declared.
4. Teams must have permission to use all submitted assets and code.
5. Projects must use Interledger or Open Payments meaningfully.
6. Teams retain ownership of their work.
7. Submissions must be suitable for public demonstration.
8. Judges' decisions are final.
9. Participants must follow the event's code of conduct.

:::note
The **full rules document** and **code of conduct** will be published here
before the hackathon begins.
:::
