# About the Hackathon

The Cape Town Software Development Meetup - Open Payments Hackathon forms part of a community-driven
initiative supported by the [Interledger Foundation](https://interledger.org/).
The event gives participants an opportunity to learn about open payment
technologies and apply them to a real South African challenge.

Over the course of the hackathon, teams will research, design and prototype
payment solutions that could help spaza shops and small informal businesses
participate more effectively in the digital economy.

:::info[Our objective]
The objective is not to produce a complete commercial product. Teams should
instead demonstrate a realistic and thoughtfully designed **proof of concept**
showing how open payment technology could address the challenge.
:::

## Key details at a glance

- **Register by:** 26 August
- **Format:** 4-week online hackathon
- **Finale:** Final in-person judging with prizes and swag
